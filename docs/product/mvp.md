# Kenya MVP

## Product outcome

A qualified creator joins, a verified organization discovers that creator, the parties agree to real work, and Wenza records delivery and the commercial outcome.

## Included in the first product

Creator and organization onboarding; permitted social account verification and metric imports; professional profiles and media kits; discovery filters; opportunity posting; applications and invitations; campaign deliverables; restricted messaging; basic matching; admin review; transaction and commission records once payment rules are agreed.

Audience commerce, gifts, subscriptions, advanced AI scoring, manager portfolio tools and expansion beyond Kenya belong to later phases.

## Delivery sequence

1. Foundation: repository, application starters, database draft, local infrastructure and local validation commands.
2. Identity and trust: decide authentication, implement secure sessions and scoped authorization, creator/organization onboarding, verification review and audit trail.
3. Creator profiles: prototype onboarding, public profiles and brand discovery; implement one approved social provider and refresh jobs.
4. Commercial workflow: opportunities, applications, invitations, campaigns and restricted messaging.
5. Matching: deterministic filters and ranking, followed by optional AI brief parsing.
6. Payments: approved provider, financial ERD, idempotent events, immutable ledger entries, commission snapshots, refunds, disputes and payouts.
7. Pilot: end-to-end workflow checks, deployment, backups, observability and curated users.
